Support

We are a small team and we read every message.

Write to us

Email hello@onelifestack.com. In the app, You > Help > Write to us opens your email app with the app and Android versions filled in, never your cards, and you can read and edit it before sending.

Common questions

Is Lots free?

Yes. There are no ads, no subscriptions and nothing to buy.

Where are my cards stored?

Only on your phone, in the app's private storage. There is no account and no cloud. We run no servers for Lots, so we cannot see your cards.

Does Lots look up opening hours or release dates?

No. Lots never goes online, so you type when a place opens and the day a film arrives. That is also why it needs no internet permission.

What if I lose or change my phone?

Use You > Back up to a file to save everything to a place you choose. On the new phone, restore from that file.

Is there a web version?

Yes: Open on the web. It works on a laptop, keeps its data in that browser only, and uses the same backup file as the phone app, so you can move your drawers between them.

How does Draw together work?

Pass the phone. Three cards are dealt, each person has one veto, and the last card standing wins the night. It all happens on the one phone.

What are Memories?

The cards you said yes to, kept like a scrapbook, with a few little stamps. There are no streaks and nothing to lose. Prefer not to keep them? Turn Memories off and cards simply go back in their drawers.

How do I install the beta?

Open the Get Lots section on your phone, tap Download, and open the file when it finishes. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser or Files app once; say yes, then tap Install. It needs Android 8.0 or newer.

Is the beta safe?

It is the same app we will put on Google Play, built by us and signed with a beta key, with the same promises: no account, no ads, no tracking, no internet permission. Check the file with the SHA-256 checksum shown next to the download, and only install it from this site.

Does it sync between devices?

Not in this version. Nothing leaves your phone unless you save a backup file.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8.0 (Oreo) and newer.

Who is it for?

Anyone aged 13 and over who has ever said "I don't mind, you choose" twice in a row.

How do I get help or share an idea?

Open You > Help in the app, or write to hello@onelifestack.com.

How do I delete everything?

You > Erase everything in the app, or just uninstall it. There is nothing to delete anywhere else.

Something went wrong?

If Lots ever closes unexpectedly, it shows an Oops screen next time with Tell us what happened. The report has the error and the versions only, never your cards.
