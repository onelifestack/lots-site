Press kit

Lots is a free, private decision app for Android. For anything else, write to hello@onelifestack.com.

In one line

When you can't decide, draw lots: keep the meals, places and films you might enjoy, and Lots draws one, quietly setting aside whatever can't happen today.

Facts

  • Name: Lots
  • Maker: OneLifeStack
  • Platform: Android 8.0 and newer, plus a web app
  • Price: free, no ads, no purchases
  • Audience: ages 13 and over
  • Privacy: no account, no analytics, no internet permission
